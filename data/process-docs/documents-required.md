# Documents & Forms Required at Each Exit Stage

Use this checklist to prepare the documents needed to complete each clearance
stage without delays.

## 1. At Resignation / Notice
- Resignation letter (via Workday).
- Acknowledgement of notice period.
- Updated personal contact details and personal email for post-exit
  correspondence.

## 2. Knowledge Transfer Stage
- **KT document** (submitted in the KT system) covering:
  - Deployment / runbook procedures
  - Access & credentials handover list
  - Pending tickets and in-flight work
  - Key contacts and vendor details
- Successor acknowledgement (signed by the receiving team member).

## 3. IT Clearance Stage
- **Asset Return Checklist** (signed by IT).
- Device handover acknowledgement.
- VPN/MFA deactivation confirmation.

## 4. Finance Clearance Stage
- No-dues declaration.
- Advance/loan settlement confirmation (if applicable).
- Bank account confirmation for FnF credit.
- Form 16 request (issued after year-end).

## 5. HR Clearance Stage
- Completed **exit interview** form.
- Signed **Confidentiality Reaffirmation** (NDA survival).
- ID badge and access card return acknowledgement.
- PF transfer/withdrawal form (Form 19 / Form 10C or UAN transfer).

## 6. On Final Sign-off
- **Relieving letter** (issued by HR).
- **Experience letter** (issued by HR).
- Full-and-final settlement statement (issued by Finance).

## Notes
- Missing documents are the most common cause of overdue stages.
- Digital signatures are accepted for all forms except statutory PF forms where
  a wet signature may be required.
