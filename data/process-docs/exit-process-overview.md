# Exit Process Overview

This guide explains what an exiting employee at Perficient goes through, the
typical timeline, and who to contact at each step.

## Step 1 — Resignation / Notice (Day 0)
- Submit your resignation to your reporting manager and HR via Workday.
- Standard notice period is **60 days** for full-time employees (30 days for
  employees with under 1 year of tenure).
- HR acknowledges and creates an **exit case** with five clearance stages.
- **Contact:** your reporting manager and your HR Business Partner.

## Step 2 — Knowledge Transfer (Weeks 1-3)
- Work with your manager to identify successors and document your work.
- Submit a Knowledge Transfer (KT) document covering deployment/runbooks,
  access & credentials, pending tickets, and key contacts.
- KT is reviewed by your manager and must be marked complete before IT
  deprovisioning of production access.
- **Contact:** reporting manager.

## Step 3 — IT Clearance (Last week)
- Return all assets (laptop, phone, YubiKey, monitors).
- System access is revoked per the IT Deprovisioning Policy; each stage targets
  completion within **5 business days**.
- **Contact:** IT Systems Administration (Sanya Kapoor).

## Step 4 — Finance Clearance (Around last working day)
- Any advances or dues are settled; full-and-final (FnF) is calculated.
- FnF is credited within **45 days** of the last working day.
- **Contact:** Finance / Payroll (Pooja Shah).

## Step 5 — HR Clearance & Documents (Last week)
- Complete the exit interview.
- Sign the confidentiality reaffirmation.
- Receive your **relieving and experience letters** after final sign-off.
- **Contact:** HR Business Partner (Meera Krishnan).

## Step 6 — Manager Sign-off (Final)
- Your manager confirms all handovers and deliverables are complete.
- Once all five stages are complete, the exit case is closed.

## SLA & Escalation
- Each stage must complete within 5 business days; overdue stages are escalated
  per the Exit SLA Policy. If a stage stalls, contact HR Operations.
