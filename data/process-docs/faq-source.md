# Exit FAQ

Common questions and answers about separation from Perficient. This document is
the source of truth for the offboarding FAQ chatbot.

## Notice Period
**Q: What is the standard notice period?**
A: 60 days for employees with 1+ year of tenure, 30 days for those under 1 year.

**Q: Can I buy out my notice period?**
A: Notice buyout is possible with manager and HR approval; the buyout amount is
recovered from your full-and-final settlement.

## Full-and-Final Settlement
**Q: When will I receive my full-and-final (FnF) settlement?**
A: FnF is processed and credited within **45 days** of your last working day,
subject to completion of all clearances and asset returns.

**Q: What does FnF include?**
A: Unpaid salary, leave encashment for eligible balance, any pending
reimbursements, minus recoveries (notice buyout, advances, unreturned assets).

## Experience & Relieving Letters
**Q: When do I get my relieving and experience letters?**
A: Both are issued by HR after the Manager Sign-off stage is complete, typically
within 3 business days of case closure.

**Q: Can I get the experience letter earlier for a background check?**
A: An employment verification letter can be requested from HR at any time; the
formal experience letter is issued at exit.

## Provident Fund (PF)
**Q: What happens to my PF?**
A: You can transfer your PF to your new employer via your UAN, or withdraw it.
HR provides the required forms during HR Clearance.

**Q: How long does PF transfer take?**
A: PF transfers typically take 15-30 days once the withdrawal/transfer form is
submitted to EPFO.

## Insurance
**Q: Does my health insurance continue after exit?**
A: Group medical insurance coverage ends on your last working day. A conversion
option to an individual policy may be available; contact HR for details.

## Access & Assets
**Q: When does my system access get revoked?**
A: Access is revoked by end of your last working day (immediately for
terminations). Return all assets on or before your last working day.

**Q: What if I can't return my laptop in time?**
A: Contact IT for a prepaid shipping kit. Unreturned assets may be recovered
from your settlement at depreciated value.

## Escalation
**Q: My clearance is stuck. What do I do?**
A: Each stage should complete within 5 business days. If overdue, contact HR
Operations; overdue stages are automatically escalated to department heads.
