# Data Retention, Confidentiality & NDA Policy

## 1. Purpose
1.1 To define confidentiality obligations that survive employment and the
retention/disposal rules for company and client data at separation.

## 2. Surviving Confidentiality Obligations
2.1 The Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) signed at joining remains binding
indefinitely after separation.
2.2 Separating employees must not retain, copy, or transmit any confidential,
proprietary, or client data.
2.3 A signed **Confidentiality Reaffirmation** is collected during HR Clearance.

## 3. Data Return & Deletion
3.1 All company data on personal devices must be deleted; the employee attests
to this in writing.
3.2 Client deliverables and source code remain the property of Perficient and/or
its clients and must be handed over during Knowledge Transfer.

## 4. Retention Schedule
4.1 Employee HR records — retained **7 years** after separation.
4.2 Payroll and tax records (PF/TDS) — retained **8 years** per statutory rules.
4.3 Email mailbox — retained **30 days** as forwarding, then archived for
**1 year**, then deleted.
4.4 Access and deprovisioning audit logs — retained **12 months**.

## 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 All work product created during employment is company IP.
5.2 Personal projects unrelated to company business are excluded per the IP
assignment clause of the employment agreement.

## 6. Breach Handling
6.1 Suspected breaches of confidentiality post-exit are referred to Legal &
Compliance and may result in legal action.

## 7. Cross-References
7.1 Asset and credential handling are governed by the IT Deprovisioning Policy.
7.2 Retention of settlement records is coordinated with Finance.
