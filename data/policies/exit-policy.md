# Employee Offboarding & Exit SLA Policy

## 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy governs the end-to-end offboarding of any employee separating
from Perficient, whether by resignation, termination, or retirement.
1.2 It defines the mandatory clearance stages, service-level agreements (SLAs),
and escalation rules to ensure timely, secure, and compliant exits.

## 2. Scope
2.1 Applies to all employees and contractors across Engineering, Finance, HR,
Sales, and IT.
2.2 Covers the period from resignation/termination acceptance through the final
full-and-final settlement.

## 3. Offboarding Stages
Every exit case must progress through the following five stages:
3.1 **IT Clearance** — Deprovisioning of all system access and recovery of
assets. Owner: IT Systems Administration.
3.2 **Finance Clearance** — Settlement of dues, recovery of advances, and
full-and-final calculation. Owner: Finance.
3.3 **HR Clearance** — Documentation, exit interview, and relieving letter.
Owner: HR Business Partner.
3.4 **Knowledge Transfer** — Handover of responsibilities, documentation, and
credentials to designated owners. Owner: Reporting Manager.
3.5 **Manager Sign-off** — Final confirmation that all deliverables and
handovers are complete. Owner: Reporting Manager.

## 4. Service-Level Agreements (SLA)
4.1 Each stage must be **completed within five (5) business days** of being
opened.
4.2 A stage is considered **OVERDUE** when its `days_open` exceeds 5.
4.3 The overall exit case must reach completion no later than the employee's
last working day, except where Finance Clearance depends on the final payroll
cycle.

## 5. Escalation Rules
5.1 **Level 1 (Day 6):** An overdue stage triggers an automated reminder to the
stage owner and the reporting manager.
5.2 **Level 2 (Day 8):** The department head is notified and the case is flagged
`at_risk`.
5.3 **Level 3 (Day 10):** Escalation to the HR Director. For any open IT
Clearance involving production or privileged access, the CISO is also notified.
5.4 Termination cases and cases involving high-criticality resources are
escalated one level earlier than the timelines above.

## 6. Roles & Responsibilities
6.1 The reporting manager is accountable for overall case completion.
6.2 HR Operations owns this policy and monitors SLA compliance across all cases.
6.3 Stage owners are responsible for updating case status promptly.

## 7. Exceptions
7.1 SLA extensions require written approval from the HR Director.
7.2 Exceptions must be logged in the exit case timeline notes.
