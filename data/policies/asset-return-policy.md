# Company Asset Return Policy

## 1. Purpose
1.1 To ensure timely and complete recovery of all company-issued assets from
separating employees.

## 2. Assets in Scope
2.1 Computing devices — laptops (MacBook Pro/Air), desktops.
2.2 Mobile devices — company iPhones and tablets.
2.3 Security hardware — YubiKeys, access cards.
2.4 Peripherals — monitors, docking stations, keyboards.
2.5 Any other company property (books, corporate credit cards, ID badge).

## 3. Return Timeline
3.1 Assets must be returned **on or before the last working day**.
3.2 For remote employees, IT arranges a prepaid shipping kit; assets must be
dispatched no later than the last working day and received within 7 days.
3.3 Failure to return assets may result in recovery deductions from the
full-and-final settlement.

## 4. Condition & Inspection
4.1 Returned devices are inspected and logged against the assets_assigned record
in the employee profile.
4.2 Damage beyond normal wear may be recovered at depreciated value.

## 5. Data Handling
5.1 Devices are backed up (where required) and then securely wiped per the IT
Deprovisioning Policy before reissue.

## 6. Sign-off
6.1 A completed asset return checklist is required to close the IT Clearance
stage of the exit case.
6.2 Any outstanding assets are flagged to Finance to hold settlement.
