# Exit Interview — Aarav Mehta (Engineering)

**Reason for leaving:**
Purely a personal growth decision. I received an opportunity to lead a larger
platform team elsewhere. This was a hard decision to make.

**What you liked:**
Almost everything about the engineering culture. Strong technical peers, real
ownership of systems, a supportive manager, and interesting problems on the
payments platform. Work-life balance was respected and on-call was reasonable.

**What could improve:**
Career ladder progression for senior ICs could be clearer, and cross-team
documentation is inconsistent. Nothing that affected my decision to leave.

**Manager feedback:**
Vikram was an excellent manager: gave me autonomy, backed me in tough calls, and
invested in my growth. I would happily work with him again.

**Would you recommend Perficient as a place to work?**
Yes, strongly, especially for engineers who want ownership and good mentorship.
