# Exit Interview — Nisha Reddy (Sales)

**Reason for leaving:**
I received an offer with clearer growth, but honestly I was also looking because
the SDR role here felt like a treadmill. Unrealistic call/quota targets and
little visibility into how to actually get promoted.

**What you liked:**
Great onboarding buddy and a friendly SDR pod. I learned a lot about outbound in
a short time and the tooling (Salesforce) was solid.

**What could improve:**
Same theme as others on the team: leadership sets aggressive targets without
enough lead support, and the pressure is relentless. Feedback in pipeline
reviews felt more like criticism than coaching. Several teammates left for the
same reasons in the last few months.

**Manager feedback:**
Karan is knowledgeable but stretched thin. One-on-ones were often cancelled and
career conversations never really happened. The activity-metric focus felt
demotivating.

**Would you recommend Perficient as a place to work?**
For the company overall, maybe. For the Sales development team, not right now
until the management and quota culture changes.
