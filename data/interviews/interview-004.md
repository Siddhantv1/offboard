# Exit Interview — Karthik Nair (Engineering)

**Reason for leaving:**
A mix of compensation and commute. My new role offers a meaningful raise and is
fully remote, which matters a lot to me right now.

**What you liked:**
Good engineering peers and interesting work on the notifications and data
platforms. Managers were generally reasonable and code review culture was
healthy.

**What could improve:**
Compensation reviews felt slow and below market for backend engineers, which was
a real factor in leaving. Also, staffing was thin — I owned two services largely
solo, which made time off stressful. Better redundancy and bus-factor planning
would help.

**Manager feedback:**
Vikram was supportive and fair, though the team was clearly under-resourced,
which limited how much support anyone could give. No complaints about him
personally.

**Would you recommend Perficient as a place to work?**
Yes, with the caveat that they should revisit backend compensation bands and
team staffing.
