# Exit Interview — Rahul Verma (Sales)

**Reason for leaving:**
Primarily burnout and frustration with sales leadership. Quotas were raised
twice in one year without any change in territory support or lead flow. I felt
set up to fail regardless of effort.

**What you liked:**
The product is genuinely good and my peers on the AE team were supportive. Some
of the enterprise clients were a pleasure to work with.

**What could improve:**
Sales management is the biggest issue. Targets are set top-down with no input,
pipeline reviews turned into blame sessions, and there was constant pressure but
little coaching. Commission plan changes were communicated late and felt unfair.
High attrition on the team is a symptom of this.

**Manager feedback:**
My manager (Karan) is under pressure himself and passes it straight down. Very
little one-on-one support or career development. Micromanaged on activity
metrics while ignoring actual deal blockers.

**Would you recommend Perficient as a place to work?**
Not in the Sales org as it currently runs. Maybe in Engineering or Product.
